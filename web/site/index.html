Sec 05Before you sendRead this first

Photo tips & PRIVACY

Photo tips

  • One clear, full-body photo — head to shoes in frame, facing the camera.
  • Daylight or bright indoor light. Avoid heavy filters and motion blur.
  • Your outfit is kept exactly as photographed — wear the look you want to walk in.
  • JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB. One person per photo.

Your photo & privacy

  • Your photo is used only to make your film — never sold, shared or used to train models.
  • We delete your photo and cut-outs 30 days after delivery.
  • Only upload a photo of yourself, or one you have permission to use.
